# Billing

We aim to make our billing processes as transparent and straightforward as our technical services. We believe you should always know exactly what you are paying for and when.

Here you will find all the practical information regarding how we handle invoicing, our accepted payment methods, and how we structure our pricing. We also detail our policies on late payments, prorated billing for mid-month changes, and the steps to take if you ever need to query a charge.
