To ensure we can continue providing dependable services to all our clients, we enforce a strict but fair late payment policy.

### Standard Terms

Unless otherwise agreed in your specific contract, all SHW Digital Services invoices carry a standard **14-day payment term**.

### The Overdue Process

1. **Friendly Reminder (Day 15):** If an invoice passes its due date, an automated, polite reminder email is sent to your billing contact.
    
2. **Second Notice (Day 21):** A follow-up email is sent, and we will attempt to call your primary contact to ensure the invoice wasn't simply missed in transit.
    
3. **Service Suspension (Day 30):** If an invoice remains unpaid for 30 days past its due date, ongoing support services and active maintenance will be paused until the account is brought up to date.
    

If you are experiencing financial difficulties, please contact us _before_ your invoice becomes overdue so we can discuss alternative arrangements.
