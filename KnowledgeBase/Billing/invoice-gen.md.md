Understanding when you will receive your invoice helps keep your business accounts organized. The billing cycle depends entirely on the type of service you are receiving from SHW Digital Services.

### One-Off Projects

For bespoke builds like simple website setups or custom automation scripts:

- **Deposit:** A 50% deposit invoice is usually generated and must be paid before any technical work or scoping begins.
    
- **Completion:** The remaining 50% balance is invoiced on the day the project is finalized and handed over to you.
    

### Monthly Retainers (Ongoing Maintenance)

If you are on an ongoing service plan (such as Active Directory administration or website maintenance):

- Invoices are generated on the 1st of every month.
    
- They are billed in advance for the upcoming month of service.
    

All invoices are sent digitally as PDF attachments to the designated billing email address provided during the initial setup.
