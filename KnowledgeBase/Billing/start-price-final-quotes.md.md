Across the SHW Digital Services website, you will see costs listed as "From £X." These are indicative starting prices, designed to give you a clear baseline before you commit time to an enquiry.

### Why Prices Are Not Fixed Immediately

Every business is different. A "simple website" for a local plumber requires drastically less technical setup than a "simple website" for a clinic that needs a secure, HIPAA-compliant patient booking portal.

### The Quoting Process

1. **The Baseline:** You see the indicative starting price (e.g., _Website builds from £150_).
    
2. **The Scope:** We discuss your specific business needs, audience, and goals.
    
3. **The Final Quote:** Based on that discussion, we provide a fixed, final price for the agreed work (e.g., _Total build cost: £450_).
    

**The final quote is the exact price you will pay.** There are no hidden fees. If a project takes us longer to build than we estimated in the final quote, we absorb that cost, not you.
