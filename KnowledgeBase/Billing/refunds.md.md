Because SHW Digital Services provides tailored, bespoke digital builds, our refund policy focuses on clear communication during the scoping phase to prevent issues before they arise.

### Project Deposits

The initial 25% deposit required for one-off projects is generally **non-refundable**. This deposit secures our time in the calendar and covers the initial architecture and planning work that begins immediately after payment.

### Account Credits

In situations where an ongoing service (like website hosting) experiences a severe outage that breaches our Service Level Agreement (SLA), we do not typically issue cash refunds. Instead, a **prorated account credit** will be applied to your account, which will automatically discount your next month's invoice.

### Cancellation Scenarios

If you must cancel a project mid-build, we will assess the work completed to date. If the value of the completed work is less than your deposit, no further billing will occur. If the work exceeds the deposit, a final invoice for the unbilled hours will be generated.
