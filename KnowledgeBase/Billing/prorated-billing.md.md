Businesses change rapidly. If you need to upgrade your services in the middle of a billing cycle, we use prorated billing to ensure you only pay for exactly what you use.

### How Prorating Works

Our standard monthly retainers run from the 1st of the month to the last day of the month.

If you request a service upgrade (for example, adding ongoing Monthly Support to an existing automation script) on the **15th** of the month:

- You will not be charged for a full month.
    
- We will calculate the cost for the remaining days of that specific month.
    
- This prorated amount will be added to your _next_ standard invoice on the 1st of the following month, alongside the regular upcoming charge.
    

This ensures you get the support you need immediately, without waiting for the next billing cycle to begin.
