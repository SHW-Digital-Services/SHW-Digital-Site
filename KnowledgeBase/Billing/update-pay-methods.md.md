If your business bank account changes, or you need to update the card you use for recurring services, it is important to update your details promptly to avoid service interruptions.

### For Direct Debit Customers

If you pay your monthly retainer via GoCardless Direct Debit:

1. Submit an enquiry stating you need to change your bank details.
    
2. We will cancel the existing mandate.
    
3. We will send you a new secure GoCardless link to set up the new bank account. _Do not send your new sort code and account number via plain text email._
    

### Changing the Billing Contact

If the person who processes your invoices leaves the business, please notify us immediately. We can update the primary billing email address on our accounting software in minutes to ensure the next invoice goes to the correct person.
