Managing which devices can connect to your company network is vital for security. Device and access policy setups start from £75 and can be tailored to your working environment.

### Company-Owned Devices

If your business provides laptops and phones, these devices should be strictly managed. Policies can enforce hard drive encryption, prevent the installation of unapproved software, and allow for remote wiping if the device is lost or stolen.

### Bring Your Own Device (BYOD)

If staff use their personal laptops or phones for work, it creates unique security challenges. We can implement "Conditional Access" policies. This means the system checks if the personal device meets specific security criteria (like having an up-to-date operating system and active antivirus) before it is allowed to open company files.

### Recommended Baseline Policies

- **Screen Lock Timeouts:** Devices should automatically lock after a short period of inactivity.
    
- **USB Drive Restrictions:** Preventing the use of unauthorized USB thumb drives to stop data leaks or malware infections.
