When an employee leaves the company, revoking their access promptly is one of the most critical security tasks for any business.

### Immediate Steps

If you are on an ongoing administration plan, please notify SHW Digital Services as soon as an exit date is confirmed. We will schedule the account deactivation to occur exactly when needed.

### The Offboarding Process

1. **Access Revocation:** The user's account is immediately disabled (we do not delete accounts immediately to preserve data), severing access to company data, emails, and VPNs.
    
2. **Session Termination:** Any active login sessions across all devices are forced to log out.
    
3. **Data Retention and Forwarding:** We can configure the departed employee's email to forward to a manager or set an auto-responder. User data (like OneDrive files) can be archived or transferred securely.
    
4. **License Reallocation:** Cost savings are achieved by unassigning paid Microsoft licenses from the disabled account so they can be reused or cancelled.
