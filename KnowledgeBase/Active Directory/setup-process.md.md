Getting your Active Directory environment established securely and correctly from day one is crucial for a dependable Microsoft environment. The starter setup begins at £200 and follows a structured timeline.

### 1. Discovery and Scoping

Before any technical work begins, we discuss your current IT landscape, how your team works, and your primary security goals. We will confirm the final scope and tailored requirements with you.

### 2. Core Architecture Build

We establish the foundational structure of your Active Directory. This includes setting up your organizational units (OUs), which group your users and devices logically (e.g., by department or location).

### 3. Baseline Security Implementation

Default security parameters are established to protect your network. This ensures the environment meets basic modern security standards before adding users, applying the principle of least privilege immediately.

### 4. Handover and Next Steps

Once the foundation is solid, we hand over the basic administrative documentation. From here, you can begin adding new users (at £15 per user) or establish more complex device policies.
