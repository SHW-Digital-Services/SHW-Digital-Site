Being unable to log in is frustrating. Before submitting a support ticket, try these common troubleshooting steps for Active Directory login failures.

### 1. Check the Basics

- **Caps Lock:** Ensure your Caps Lock key is not accidentally turned on. Passwords are case-sensitive.
    
- **Network Connection:** Active Directory needs to communicate with the server. Verify your computer is connected to the internet or the company network (via Wi-Fi or a physical cable).
    
- **VPN:** If you are working remotely, ensure your Virtual Private Network (VPN) is connected prior to attempting to log in, if your company requires it.
    

### 2. Account Lockouts

If you enter the wrong password too many times, Active Directory will automatically lock your account to protect against hacking attempts.

- Wait 15 to 30 minutes. Many policies are set to automatically unlock after a short duration.
    
- If you are still locked out, you will need to contact an administrator to have the account manually unlocked and the password reset.
    

### 3. "Trust Relationship" Error

If you see an error stating _"The trust relationship between this workstation and the primary domain failed,"_ your computer has lost its secure connection to the network. Do not attempt to fix this yourself; it requires an administrator to rejoin the device to the domain.
