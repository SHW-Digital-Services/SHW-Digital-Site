Managing an Active Directory environment is not a "set and forget" task. Our ongoing administration packages, starting from £90 per month, ensure your network remains dependable, secure, and up-to-date.

### What is Usually Included?

While every contract is holistic and tailored to your specific business needs, a typical monthly administration package covers:

- **Routine Health Checks:** Proactive monitoring of directory replication and server health to catch issues before they cause downtime.
    
- **Security Patching:** Applying critical updates to the Active Directory servers.
    
- **Standard Policy Adjustments:** Making minor tweaks to Group Policies or device access rules as your business needs evolve.
    
- **Stale Account Cleanup:** Disabling computer and user accounts that have been inactive for a prolonged period to maintain security hygiene.
    

### What is Excluded?

Major structural changes (like merging with another company's network) or high-volume tasks (like setting up 20 new users at once) are generally scoped as separate mini-projects outside the standard monthly retainer. The exact inclusions will always be confirmed before the agreement begins.
