Over time, as employees change roles, take on temporary projects, or cover for colleagues on leave, they accumulate access to folders and systems they no longer need. This is known as "permission creep."

### Why You Need a Permissions Review

Leaving unnecessary access open creates a significant security risk. If that user's account is ever compromised, the attacker has access to far more sensitive company data than they should.

### What the Review Covers

Starting from £95, a policy and permissions review involves a systematic audit of your Active Directory.

1. **Group Audit:** We review who is in which security group.
    
2. **Folder Access:** We check who has read, write, or delete permissions for highly sensitive shared drives (like HR or Finance folders).
    
3. **Stale Accounts:** We identify user and computer accounts that haven't logged in for a prolonged period, which should be disabled to prevent them from being hijacked.
    

You will receive a clear report of current access levels, allowing management to confirm if access should remain or be securely revoked.
