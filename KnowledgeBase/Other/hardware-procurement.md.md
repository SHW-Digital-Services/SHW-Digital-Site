A common question we receive from our IT administration clients is whether we supply physical hardware, such as laptops, monitors, or servers.

### Our Stance on Hardware

**SHW Digital Services is a digital consultancy, not a hardware reseller.** We do not sell laptops, phones, or network cables directly. This allows us to remain completely unbiased when recommending technology.

### How We Help

Instead of selling you marked-up equipment, we provide expert procurement advice. If you need to buy 10 new laptops for your sales team, we will:

1. Assess the software they need to run.
    
2. Provide you with the exact technical specifications required (e.g., RAM, processor speed, hard drive size).
    
3. Recommend trusted direct vendors (like Dell or Lenovo) so you can purchase the hardware directly using your own business accounts.
