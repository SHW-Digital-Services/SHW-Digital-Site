We don't just build technology and walk away. A new tool is only valuable if your team actually knows how to use it confidently.

### Custom Workshops

If we have just built a new CRM automation for you, or migrated your team to Microsoft 365, we can provide bespoke, remote training sessions.

- **Format:** Typically conducted via Microsoft Teams or Zoom.
    
- **Recording:** We always record these sessions and provide you with the MP4 file, allowing you to use the video as a training resource for future hires.
    

### Scope and Cost
