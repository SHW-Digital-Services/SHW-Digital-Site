Technology should be usable by everyone, regardless of their physical or cognitive abilities. At SHW Digital Services, we integrate accessibility into our core development practices.

### Web Accessibility Standards

When we build websites and client portals, we strive to adhere to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 AA standards. This includes ensuring:

- **Color Contrast:** Text is easily readable against background colors for users with visual impairments.
    
- **Keyboard Navigation:** Interactive elements (like forms and buttons) can be fully operated using just a keyboard.
    
- **Screen Reader Support:** Semantic HTML and Alt Text are used extensively so visually impaired users can navigate the site using screen reading software.
    

If your specific industry requires strict AAA compliance or specialized accessibility audits, please let us know during the project scoping phase.
