Having automated backups is essential, but it is only half the battle. If your office flooded today, do you know exactly how long it would take to get your business back online?

### What is a Disaster Recovery Plan (DRP)?

A DRP is a formal document that dictates exactly what steps must be taken in the event of a catastrophic system failure, ransomware attack, or physical disaster.

We can consult with your management team to help you define two critical metrics:

- **Recovery Time Objective (RTO):** The maximum acceptable amount of time your systems can be offline before the business suffers irreversible damage.
    
- **Recovery Point Objective (RPO):** The maximum amount of data (measured in time) you can afford to lose (e.g., losing 1 hour of emails vs. 24 hours of emails).
    

Defining these metrics allows us to architect the correct backup and failover systems to meet your specific business requirements.
