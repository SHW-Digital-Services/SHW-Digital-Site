Not every process in a business needs to be automated, but recognizing the right tasks can save your team hours of repetitive work.

### What Makes a Task a Good Candidate?

When looking for tasks to automate, look for these three characteristics:

1. **Highly Repetitive:** Tasks that are done exactly the same way every day or week (e.g., copying invoice data from an email into a spreadsheet).
    
2. **Rule-Based:** If a task relies on strict logic (e.g., "If column A says 'Paid', move the file to Folder B") rather than human judgment, a script can easily handle it.
    
3. **Prone to Human Error:** Data entry across multiple systems is tedious and often leads to typos. Scripts copy and paste data perfectly every time.
