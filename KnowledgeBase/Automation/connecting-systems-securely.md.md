Getting two independent software systems to talk to each other is one of the most powerful ways to automate your business. System integration starts from £50 as an add-on.

### Secure Data Transfer

When we build a script to connect systems (for example, taking a new lead from your website and putting it into your CRM), security is paramount.

- **Encrypted Connections:** Scripts use secure protocols (HTTPS/TLS) to ensure data cannot be intercepted while in transit.
    
- **Authentication:** We utilize secure API keys or modern authentication methods (like OAuth) rather than storing plain-text passwords in the script.
    

By connecting systems directly, you eliminate the security risk of staff downloading sensitive spreadsheets to their local computers just to upload them somewhere else.
