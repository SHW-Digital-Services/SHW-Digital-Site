Businesses often receive data in messy, unreadable formats. Manually cleaning this data—like fixing dates, standardizing phone numbers, or extracting specific text—is tedious.

### How Data Parsing Works

Data parsing scripts take a raw input (like a massive CSV export from an old software system) and automatically format it into exactly what you need.

- **Cleaning:** Removing empty spaces, deleting duplicate entries, or stripping out unwanted special characters.
    
- **Extraction:** Pulling just the postcodes out of a long paragraph of text.
    
- **Transformation:** Changing date formats from American (MM/DD/YYYY) to UK standard (DD/MM/YYYY) instantly across thousands of rows.
    

These practical scripts ensure that by the time a human needs to look at the data, it is clean, accurate, and ready to use.
