Communication is vital, but manually typing and sending the same routine emails every week is a drain on your resources.

### Practical Email Automations

We can build scripts that connect to your business email (like Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace) to automate standard communications based on specific triggers.

- **Onboarding:** Automatically sending a welcome pack and setup instructions exactly 10 minutes after a new client is added to your database.
    
- **Reminders:** Scanning a spreadsheet for upcoming renewal dates and sending a reminder email 30 days before the contract expires.
    
- **Report Distribution:** A script that automatically attaches a weekly PDF report and emails it to the management team every Friday at 5:00 PM.
    

Automating these workflows ensures your clients always receive prompt, consistent communication while giving time back to the people running the business.
