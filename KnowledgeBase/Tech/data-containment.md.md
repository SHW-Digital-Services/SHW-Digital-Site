If your business suspects a data breach—whether it is a compromised email account, a ransomware alert, or unusual activity in your CRM—immediate and decisive action is required to contain the damage.

### The Immediate Response

Your primary goal in the first hour is containment, not investigation.

1. **Isolate the Threat:** Disconnect the affected computer or server from the network immediately. Pull the physical Ethernet cable or disable the Wi-Fi. **Do not turn the machine off**, as this can destroy volatile memory evidence needed by forensic investigators later.
    
2. **Revoke Access:** Force a global password reset and terminate all active login sessions for the compromised user account across all company platforms (Microsoft 365, CRMs, financial software).
    
3. **Halt Automations:** Pause any automated data syncing scripts or backup schedules to prevent the corrupted data or malware from being copied into your secure backups.
    

### Communication and Reporting

Once the immediate bleeding has stopped, the secondary phase begins.

- **Engage Support:** Contact your IT provider or SHW Digital Services immediately so we can begin auditing logs to determine the extent of the breach.
    
- **Compliance:** Remember that under UK GDPR, you have a strict 72-hour window to report significant data breaches to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO).
