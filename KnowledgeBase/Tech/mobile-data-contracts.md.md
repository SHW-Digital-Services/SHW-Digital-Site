As your business grows, managing individual mobile phone and data contracts for staff becomes inefficient and costly. Consolidating your telecommunications strategy requires careful evaluation.

### Assessing Your True Usage

Before approaching providers for a business fleet contract, you need an accurate picture of what your team actually uses.

- **Data Dominance:** Voice minutes are rarely the bottleneck anymore. Focus heavily on average monthly data consumption.
    
- **The "Unlimited" Trap:** Many providers offer "unlimited" data, but hide strict fair-use policies or speed throttling clauses in the fine print. Ensure the tethering limit (using a phone as a hotspot for a laptop) is sufficient for field staff.
    

### Network Coverage and Reliability

A cheap contract is useless if your staff cannot get a signal.

- **Multi-Network Sims:** For critical field operations, consider M2M (Machine to Machine) or roaming SIM cards that can automatically switch between major networks (e.g., O2, EE, Vodafone) depending on which has the strongest signal in a specific area.
    
- **Hardware Allowances:** Decide if your business model is better suited to a SIM-only contract (where staff use their own devices or the business buys handsets outright) versus a bundled contract where the cost of the hardware is leased over 24 months.
