Startups are frequent targets for cyberattacks because hackers know they often lack the enterprise-grade defenses of larger corporations. Establishing a solid security baseline from day one is far cheaper than recovering from a breach.

### The Non-Negotiable Baseline

If you only implement three security measures, make it these:

1. **Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA):** Enforce MFA on _every_ company account, especially email (Microsoft 365 / Google Workspace) and your financial software. A stolen password is useless to a hacker if they do not also have the staff member's phone.
    
2. **Principle of Least Privilege:** Do not make every founder an IT Administrator. Create standard user accounts for daily work, and only use an Admin account when specifically required to change system settings.
    
3. **Automated Patching:** Configure all company laptops and servers to automatically install security updates and operating system patches overnight. Unpatched software is the easiest entry point for malware.
    

Implementing these three steps eliminates the vast majority of automated, opportunistic cyber threats.
