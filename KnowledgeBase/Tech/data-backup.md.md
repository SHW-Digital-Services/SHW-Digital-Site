A business without a robust backup strategy is only one hardware failure or ransomware attack away from catastrophe. Simply dragging files onto a USB drive occasionally is not a backup plan.

### The 3-2-1 Backup Rule

The industry standard for reliable data protection is the 3-2-1 rule. You should always have:

- **3 Copies of your data:** The original live production data, plus two backups.
    
- **2 Different media types:** For example, keeping one backup on a local physical server or Network Attached Storage (NAS) drive, and another in a different format.
    
- **1 Offsite copy:** This is the most critical step. At least one backup must live securely in the cloud (like Azure or AWS) or in a physical location completely separate from your main office. If your office suffers a fire or theft, your offsite backup ensures the business survives.
    

### Testing is Mandatory

A backup is worthless if it cannot be restored. SHW Digital Services highly recommends scheduling quarterly "test restores" to ensure the data is uncorrupted and can be retrieved quickly in an emergency.
