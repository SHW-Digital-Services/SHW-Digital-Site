If you have forgotten your password or simply want to update it for security reasons, you can reset it easily from the main login screen.

### The Reset Process

1. Navigate to the Client Portal login page.
    
2. Click the **"Forgot Password?"** link located beneath the login form.
    
3. Enter the email address associated with your account.
    
4. Check your inbox for a password reset link (this link expires after 15 minutes for your security).
    
5. Click the link and enter your new password.
    

### Account Lockouts

For security reasons, your account will temporarily lock after five consecutive failed login attempts. If this happens, please wait 15 minutes before trying again or attempting a password reset.
