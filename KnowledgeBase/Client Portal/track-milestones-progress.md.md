We believe in complete transparency while we are building your digital services. The Client Portal allows you to see exactly where your project stands at any given moment.

### The Project Dashboard

When you open an active project in the portal, you will see a milestone tracker. This breaks the build down into practical stages, such as:

- Initial Architecture
    
- Development & Coding
    
- Testing & QA
    
- Handover
    

As our team completes a phase, the progress bar updates automatically. You can also view the estimated delivery date and any outstanding tasks that require your attention (such as providing final website copy).
