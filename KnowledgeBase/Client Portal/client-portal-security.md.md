Protecting your business data is our top priority. We use the Client Portal specifically because it offers a much higher standard of security than managing projects via standard email.

### How Your Data is Protected

- **Encryption:** All data transferred between your browser and the portal is secured using enterprise-grade TLS encryption.
    
- **Isolated Storage:** Your project files and billing information are stored in secure, isolated database environments.
    
- **Authentication:** The portal uses modern, secure session management to prevent unauthorized access.
    

By keeping all project scopes, invoices, and file transfers inside the portal, we significantly reduce the risk of phishing attacks or intercepted communications.
