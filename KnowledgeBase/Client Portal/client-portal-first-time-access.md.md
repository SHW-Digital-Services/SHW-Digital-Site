The SHW Digital Services Client Portal is your central hub for managing your projects, viewing invoices, and securely communicating with our team.

### Your Invitation

When you begin your first project or sign up for an ongoing retainer, you will receive an email invitation to access the portal.

1. Click the secure link within the invitation email.
    
2. You will be prompted to create a strong password. (We highly recommend using a passphrase).
    
3. Once logged in, please take a moment to review your contact details and ensure they are correct.
    

### Bookmark the Portal

For quick access in the future, we recommend bookmarking the portal login page in your web browser. If you ever lose the link, you can always find a direct login button on the main SHW Digital Services website.
