# Client Portal

The SHW Digital Services Client Portal is your secure, centralized hub for interacting with our team. It is designed to make managing your projects and support requests as easy as possible.

This section provides a guide to getting the most out of the portal. Learn how to log in for the first time, submit support tickets, securely upload sensitive files, and review and approve project scopes. By keeping our communications within this secure environment, we protect your data and ensure your requests are handled efficiently.
