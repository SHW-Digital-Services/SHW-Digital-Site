While all invoices are emailed to your primary billing contact, the Client Portal retains a complete, permanent history of your financial transactions with SHW Digital Services.

### Locating Your Invoices

1. Log in to the portal and select the **Billing** tab from the main menu.
    
2. Here, you will see a list of all past and current invoices, categorized by status (Paid, Unpaid, Overdue).
    
3. Click on any invoice number to view the itemized breakdown.
    

### Downloading for Your Records

To download a PDF copy of an invoice or a payment receipt for your own accounting software, click the **Download PDF** icon next to the relevant transaction. You can also view upcoming estimated charges for your next monthly retainer billing cycle.
