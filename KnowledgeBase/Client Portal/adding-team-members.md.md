If you have an ongoing contract with us, you may want other members of your business to have access to the Client Portal (for example, allowing your finance manager to download invoices).

### Requesting Access for a Colleague

Currently, to maintain strict security over who can view your project scopes and billing data, new portal users must be authorized by the primary account holder.

1. Submit a quick support ticket through the portal requesting a new user.
    
2. Provide the colleague's full name, email address, and required role (e.g., Billing Only, Support Only, or Full Access).
    
3. We will provision the account and send them a secure invitation link directly.
