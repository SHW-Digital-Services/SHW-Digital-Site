During a website build or script setup, we will often need you to provide files like company logos, PDF documents, or test data. Sending large or sensitive files over plain-text email is unsecure and inefficient.

### Using the Secure Upload Feature

1. Navigate to the **Files** section of your active project in the portal.
    
2. Drag and drop your files into the upload area.
    
3. The portal securely encrypts these files in transit and at rest.
    

### File Requirements

Please ensure any test data you provide for automation scripts does _not_ contain real, sensitive client information (like live credit card numbers). Always provide "dummy" data for testing purposes.
