You have control over how and when SHW Digital Services contacts you. You can adjust your notification settings directly within the portal.

### Setting Your Preferences

1. Click on your profile icon in the top right corner of the portal.
    
2. Select **Account Settings** and navigate to **Notifications**.
    
3. Here, you can toggle which emails you receive.
    

### Critical Communications

Please note that while you can opt out of marketing or general update emails, you cannot opt out of critical system alerts, invoice deliveries, or security breach notifications, as these are essential for the safe management of your account.
