SHW Digital Services operates entirely paperless. All project scopes, maintenance agreements, and Change Orders are reviewed and signed digitally.

### How to Sign a Contract

1. When a contract or scope is ready for your review, you will receive an email notification linking you to the Client Portal.
    
2. Log in to view the full PDF document securely.
    
3. Click the **"Approve and Sign"** button at the bottom of the document.
    
4. The system will record your user credential, IP address, and a timestamp to create a legally binding electronic signature.
    

Once signed, a finalized PDF copy of the contract is automatically stored in your portal's Document library for your permanent records.
