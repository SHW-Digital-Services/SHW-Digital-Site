A Service Level Agreement (SLA) is a core part of our ongoing maintenance contracts, guaranteeing how quickly we will respond to your support requests.

### Response Times

Our standard SLAs are categorized by priority:

- **Critical (e.g., Server down, website offline):** Response within 1 business hour.
    
- **High (e.g., Core automation script failing):** Response within 4 business hours.
    
- **Normal (e.g., Requesting a new user setup):** Response within 1-2 business days.
    

### Business Hours

Unless you have a specific 24/7 support add-on in your contract, all SLA response times are calculated during standard UK business hours (Monday to Friday, 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM). Tickets submitted outside these hours are logged immediately but SLA timers begin the following business morning.
