We believe in providing excellent service that you want to keep, rather than locking you into lengthy, restrictive agreements.

### Monthly Retainers

Most ongoing services, such as website maintenance or Active Directory administration, operate on a **rolling 30-day contract**.

- **Cancellation:** If you wish to cancel an ongoing service, we require a standard 30-day written notice. This allows us time to securely hand over administrative credentials and wind down monitoring services safely.
    

### One-Off Projects

If you need to cancel a one-off build (like an automation script) after the contract is signed but before completion, you will only be liable for the work completed up to the date of cancellation. The initial 50% project deposit is generally non-refundable to cover the architecture phase.
