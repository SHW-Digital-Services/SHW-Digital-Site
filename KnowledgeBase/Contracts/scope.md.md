Before you sign any contract or pay any invoice with SHW Digital Services, we go through a mandatory scoping phase. This ensures both parties are completely aligned on the expected outcome.

### What Happens During Scoping?

1. **Discovery:** We meet (via call or email) to discuss your business problem, goals, and technical environment.
    
2. **Drafting:** We draft a plain-English document detailing exactly what we will build, what we won't build, and what resources we need from you.
    
3. **Fixed Pricing:** Based on this exact scope, we calculate a final, fixed price.
    

By finalizing the scope _before_ the contract is signed, we eliminate the risk of "scope creep" and ensure you never encounter unexpected bills.
