Digital projects are a collaborative effort. While we handle the technical heavy lifting, our standard contracts outline specific prerequisites required from you to ensure the project stays on schedule.

### Common Client Responsibilities

Depending on your project, you may be contractually required to provide:

- **Timely Feedback:** Reviewing scopes, designs, or test scripts within an agreed timeframe (usually 3-5 business days).
    
- **Access Credentials:** Providing administrative logins to your domain registrar, Microsoft 365 environment, or CRM.
    
- **Content and Assets:** Supplying final text copy, company logos, and brand guidelines before the development phase begins.
    

If a project is delayed significantly because prerequisites have not been provided, the estimated delivery date on your contract will be pushed back accordingly.
