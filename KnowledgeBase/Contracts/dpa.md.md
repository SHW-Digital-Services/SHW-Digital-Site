As a UK-based business, SHW Digital Services strictly adheres to the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018.

### Data Processing

When we build automations, websites, or manage your Active Directory, we act as a "Data Processor" on your behalf. Our contracts explicitly state that:

- We only process data according to your documented instructions.
    
- We ensure all personnel handling the data are bound by confidentiality.
    
- We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to ensure data security.
    

### Test Data

To minimize risk, we require clients to provide "dummy" or anonymized data during the testing phase of automation scripts. We will never request live databases containing sensitive Personal Identifiable Information (PII) unless absolutely necessary for final deployment.
