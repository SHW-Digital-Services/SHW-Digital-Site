A common concern when hiring a digital consultancy is understanding who actually owns the final product once it is built.

### Transfer of Ownership

At SHW Digital Services, the rule is simple: **Once your final invoice is paid in full, the intellectual property of the custom deliverables transfers entirely to you.**

- **Websites:** You own the design, content, and the website itself.
    
- **Scripts:** You own the custom automation code we wrote for your specific workflow.
    

### Open Source and Third-Party Tools

The only exception is if your project incorporates pre-existing open-source libraries or third-party software (like a Microsoft 365 license or a Supabase backend). In these cases, you own the _configuration_ and the _custom code_, while the third-party platform retains ownership of their underlying proprietary software.
