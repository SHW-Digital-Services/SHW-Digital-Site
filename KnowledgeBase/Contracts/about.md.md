# Contracts

Clear agreements are the foundation of any successful digital partnership. At SHW Digital Services, we believe in complete transparency before, during, and after a project is built.

This section covers the standard terms that govern how we work with our clients. You will find information on everything from how we protect your sensitive data through Non-Disclosure Agreements, to how we define our Service Level Agreements (SLAs) for ongoing support.

We design our contracts to be easy to understand and fair. Whether you are signing up for a rolling monthly retainer or a fixed-price bespoke project, these articles outline your rights, our responsibilities, and how we handle changes along the way.
