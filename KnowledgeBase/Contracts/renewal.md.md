We aim to make ongoing administration and maintenance as seamless as possible so you can focus on running your business without worrying about expiring contracts.

### Automated Rolling Renewals

Most standard monthly support packages operate on an evergreen, rolling basis. This means your contract automatically renews on the 1st of every month without requiring fresh signatures, ensuring there is no lapse in your server monitoring or security patching.

### Annual Reviews

While the contract rolls monthly, we recommend an annual account review. During this review, we will:

- Assess if your current support tier still matches your business headcount.
    
- Discuss any changes to your technical infrastructure.
    
- Provide 30 days' advance notice of any upcoming adjustments to base retainer pricing due to inflation or changing software costs.
