To ensure your website build stays on schedule, having your content prepared and formatted correctly before development begins is essential.

### How to Provide Text Content

Please provide all written website copy in a single, well-organized digital document (like Microsoft Word or Google Docs).

- Use clear headings to indicate which text belongs on which page (e.g., "Home Page - About Section").
    
- Ensure all text has been proofread for spelling and grammar, as our developers will copy and paste the text exactly as provided.
    

### How to Provide Images

- **Resolution:** Provide the highest quality original images you have. We will handle the compression and resizing for web optimization.
    
- **File Transfer:** Do not send images embedded inside a Word document or via WhatsApp, as this degrades the quality. Please upload images directly to the secure folder we provide in your Client Portal, or share a Dropbox/Google Drive link.
    
- **Copyright:** You must own the rights to the images you provide, or have purchased the appropriate stock licenses. We cannot use images saved directly from a Google Image search.
