Understanding how visitors interact with your website allows you to make informed decisions about your marketing and business strategy.

### Standard Analytics Integration

During the build phase, SHW Digital Services can integrate your preferred analytics tracking code into your website's header. We typically work with two main options:

1. **Google Analytics (GA4):** The industry standard. It provides deep, highly detailed insights into user demographics, behavior flows, and conversion tracking. However, it requires a robust cookie consent banner to comply with UK GDPR.
    
2. **Privacy-Focused Alternatives:** We also integrate lightweight tools like Plausible or Fathom Analytics. These provide excellent top-level metrics (visitor counts, most popular pages, referral sources) without tracking individual user data, meaning you often do not need to display an intrusive cookie banner.
    

Once integrated, you will retain full administrative access to your analytics dashboard to monitor your traffic independently.
