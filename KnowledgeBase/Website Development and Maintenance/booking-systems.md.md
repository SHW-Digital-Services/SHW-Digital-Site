Allowing clients to book appointments directly through your website reduces administrative overhead and provides a frictionless customer experience.

### Integration Options

We do not usually recommend building a booking engine from scratch, as it is costly and difficult to maintain. Instead, we integrate industry-standard, secure third-party tools directly into your site's design:

- **Microsoft Bookings:** Ideal if your business already operates on a Microsoft 365 license, as it syncs directly with your Outlook calendar.
    
- **Calendly:** A highly popular, user-friendly option that integrates seamlessly with both Google Workspace and Microsoft environments.
    
- **Industry-Specific Tools:** If you are a clinic using specialized software (like Cliniko or Mindbody), we can embed their native booking widgets into your new website pages.
    

### How it Works

When a client clicks "Book Now," a stylized calendar appears. They select an available time (based on your live availability), and the system automatically sends calendar invites to both you and the client.
