Your domain name (e.g., www.yourbusiness.com) is your digital real estate. Managing it correctly is crucial to ensuring your website and emails remain online.

### Domain Ownership

SHW Digital Services strongly believes that clients should always retain total ownership of their domain names. We will guide you on how to purchase your domain through a reputable registrar (like GoDaddy, Namecheap, or Cloudflare) using your own business details and credit card. This ensures you are never held hostage by a web developer.

### Understanding DNS (Domain Name System)

Think of DNS as the address book of the internet. When someone types your domain into their browser, the DNS records tell the browser exactly which server to look at to find your website.

- During a website launch, we will need temporary access to your domain registrar to update these DNS records (specifically the "A Record" and "CNAME") to point your domain to our secure hosting servers.
