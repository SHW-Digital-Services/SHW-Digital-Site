Search Engine Optimization (SEO) ensures that Google and other search engines can read, understand, and rank your website when potential clients search for your services.

### Our Baseline SEO Setup

Every website built by SHW Digital Services includes foundational technical SEO:

- **Meta Titles and Descriptions:** We write concise, keyword-relevant descriptions that appear on the Google search results page.
    
- **Semantic HTML:** We structure your website using correct heading tags (H1, H2, H3) so search engine bots can easily read your content hierarchy.
    
- **Image Optimization:** All uploaded images are compressed for speed and tagged with "Alt Text," which describes the image to visually impaired users and search engines.
    
- **Mobile Responsiveness:** Google heavily penalizes websites that do not work well on mobile phones. Our sites are guaranteed to be mobile-friendly.
    

Please note that while we provide this strong technical foundation, ranking on the first page of Google for highly competitive keywords requires ongoing content marketing and external SEO efforts over many months.
