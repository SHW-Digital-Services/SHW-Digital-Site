An SSL (Secure Sockets Layer) certificate is no longer an optional extra; it is a mandatory requirement for operating a professional business website.

### What is an SSL Certificate?

An SSL certificate encrypts the data sent between your website and your visitor's browser. You can tell a site has an SSL certificate because the web address starts with "HTTPS" instead of "HTTP," and a small padlock icon appears next to the URL.

### Why You Need It

1. **Data Security:** It prevents hackers from intercepting sensitive information, such as passwords or contact form submissions.
    
2. **Browser Trust:** Modern web browsers (like Chrome and Safari) will display a prominent "Not Secure" warning to users if they try to visit a site without an SSL certificate, which drastically damages brand trust.
    
3. **Search Rankings:** Google officially uses HTTPS as a ranking signal, meaning secure sites rank higher in search results than unsecured ones.
