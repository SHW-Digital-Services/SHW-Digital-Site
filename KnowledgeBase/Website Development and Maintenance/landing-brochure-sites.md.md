When scoping a new web project, it is important to choose the right format for your specific business goals. We typically categorize small business builds into two distinct types.

### The Brochure Website

A brochure site acts as your complete digital storefront. It usually consists of multiple pages (Home, About Us, Services, Case Studies, Contact).

- **Goal:** To build brand authority, educate visitors about everything your business does, and act as a reliable reference point for potential clients who are researching you.
    

### The Landing Page

A landing page is a single, highly focused web page designed for a specific marketing campaign.

- **Goal:** Conversion. A landing page strips away the main navigation menus and distractions. It focuses the visitor entirely on one single "Call to Action" (CTA), such as downloading a whitepaper, signing up for a webinar, or requesting a quote for a specific product.
    
- **Usage:** These are ideal when you are running paid advertisements (like Google Ads or Facebook Ads) where you want to maximize the return on your ad spend.
