As your business expands and you introduce new services or team members, your website will naturally need to grow alongside it.

### The Request Process

If you require a new page added to your existing website:

1. **Submit a Request:** Open a support ticket via the Client Portal detailing the goal of the new page.
    
2. **Provide Content:** Upload the final text copy and any specific images you want featured on the page.
    
3. **Scoping and Approval:** We will review the content and provide a fixed quote for the design, build, and integration of the new page (including adding it to your main navigation menus).
    

Once you digitally approve the scope and cost, the new page will be built in our staging environment for your review before being pushed live to your public website.
