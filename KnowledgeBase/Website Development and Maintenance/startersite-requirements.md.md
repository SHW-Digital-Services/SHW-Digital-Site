A single-page starter site is an excellent, cost-effective way to establish your digital presence quickly. To ensure a smooth build, we require a few key assets from you before development begins.

### Essential Elements Required

1. **Branding Assets:** High-resolution versions of your company logo (preferably with a transparent background in PNG or SVG format) and your official brand color codes.
    
2. **The "Hero" Statement:** A strong, one-sentence headline for the very top of the website that clearly explains what you do and who you do it for.
    
3. **About Us / Services Copy:** A short paragraph explaining your business, and a bulleted list of the core services you offer.
    
4. **Contact Information:** The primary email address, phone number, and physical address (if applicable) that you want potential clients to use to reach you.
    

Once we have these four elements, our team can rapidly design and deploy your starter site.
