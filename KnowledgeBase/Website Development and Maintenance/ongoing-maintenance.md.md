A website is not a static brochure; it is a piece of living software. To remain secure and functional, it requires proactive technical upkeep.

### Core Maintenance Retainer Inclusions

Our monthly website maintenance plans give you total peace of mind by handling the technical backend of your site:

- **Software Updates:** We safely apply updates to your core Content Management System (CMS), visual themes, and third-party plugins. We test these updates in a staging environment first to ensure they don't break your live site.
    
- **Security Scanning:** Daily automated scans to detect and quarantine malware or unauthorized file changes.
    
- **Automated Backups:** Secure, off-site backups of your database and files so we can restore your site immediately in the event of a critical failure.
    
- **Uptime Monitoring:** Our systems check your website every few minutes. If the site goes offline, our support team is alerted instantly.
    

By letting us handle the technical upkeep, you can focus on running your business.
