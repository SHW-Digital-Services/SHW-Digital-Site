You do not need a professional television studio to host a great webinar, but relying on your laptop's built-in hardware can result in echoey audio and grainy video.

### Audio is Priority Number One

Audiences will forgive a slightly blurry camera, but they will leave immediately if the audio is bad.

- **USB Microphones:** We highly recommend a dedicated USB microphone (like the Blue Yeti, Elgato Wave, or a reliable headset) placed close to the speaker.
    
- **Room Environment:** Present in a room with soft furnishings (carpets, curtains) rather than empty spaces with hard floors, which cause audio bounce and echo.
    

### Webcam Basics

- **External Cameras:** A 1080p external USB webcam (like a Logitech C920) is a massive step up from standard laptop cameras.
    
- **Lighting:** Ensure your primary light source (a window or a ring light) is positioned _in front_ of you, lighting your face. Never sit with your back to a bright window.
