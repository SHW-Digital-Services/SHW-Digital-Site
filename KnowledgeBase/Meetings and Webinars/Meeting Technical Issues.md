Even with perfect preparation, live internet broadcasts can occasionally encounter turbulence. SHW Digital Services provides calm, professional support when things go wrong.

### The Support Co-Host Role

During your live event, an SHW technician remains on the call as a hidden Co-Host.

- **Audio Drops:** If your microphone stops working, we will immediately step in via the chat, alert you, and assist with switching your audio input.
    
- **Trolls and Disruptions:** If an attendee manages to disrupt the meeting (unmuting, inappropriate chat), our technician will instantly remove them and lock the room.
    
- **Slide Failures:** We always keep a backup copy of your presentation slides. If your computer crashes, we can seamlessly take over the screen sharing on your behalf.
