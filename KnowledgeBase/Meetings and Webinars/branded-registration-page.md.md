Your webinar registration page is often the first interaction an attendee has with your brand. It should look professional and capture the right information.

### Branding Options

Depending on the webinar platform we are using (such as Zoom or Microsoft Teams), we can customize the registration portal to match your company identity:

- **Logos and Banners:** Uploading your high-resolution company logo and a custom header image.
    
- **Color Schemes:** Adjusting buttons and text links to align with your brand guidelines.
    
- **Custom Questions:** Beyond just capturing names and emails, we can add custom fields to ask attendees about their job titles, industries, or specific questions they want answered during the Q&A segment.
