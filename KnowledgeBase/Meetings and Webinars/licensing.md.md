Choosing the right software platform is critical to the success of your online event. We support a variety of industry-standard tools.

### Supported Platforms

- **Zoom (Meetings & Webinars):** Highly reliable, universally recognized, and best for events that require extensive breakout rooms or robust registration pages.
    
- **Microsoft Teams:** The best choice for internal company Town Halls if your business is already heavily invested in the Microsoft 365 ecosystem.
    
- **Google Meet:** Ideal for fast, frictionless browser-based meetings where attendees do not want to download software.
    

### Licensing

If your business does not hold an expensive "Webinar500" license, you do not need to buy one for a one-off event. We can host the event on SHW Digital Services' enterprise-grade licenses, providing you access to premium features without the annual subscription costs.
