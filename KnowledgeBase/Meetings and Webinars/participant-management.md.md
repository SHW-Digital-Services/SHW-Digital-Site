Nothing ruins a professional presentation faster than background noise, barking dogs, or typing sounds from an unmuted attendee.

### Proactive Muting

When managing your event, SHW Digital Services implements strict room controls.

- **Mute on Entry:** We configure the software so every attendee enters the room with their microphone already turned off.
    
- **Restricting Unmuting:** For large broadcasts, we disable the ability for attendees to unmute themselves entirely. If a specific attendee needs to speak during a Q&A, we will manually grant them microphone permission.
    

### Handling Disruptions

If an attendee accidentally unmutes or causes a disruption, our background technicians will instantly mute them. If the disruption is intentional, the user will be securely removed from the meeting and banned from re-entering.
