We offer two distinct levels of support depending on the size and formality of your online event.

### Small Meeting Support

Designed for interactive workshops, board meetings, or client pitches (usually under 30 people).

- **Format:** Everyone is on camera and can unmute themselves.
    
- **Our Role:** We manage the waiting room, help attendees struggling with their audio, and manage the screen sharing so the host can focus on the conversation.
    

### Full Webinar Hosting

Designed for large, broadcast-style presentations (50 to 500+ people).

- **Format:** Only the host and panelists are on camera. Attendees are automatically muted and interact only via Q&A and Chat boxes.
    
- **Our Role:** We manage the complex backend registration, run the pre-show tech checks, moderate the Q&A queue, and ensure the broadcast stays stable under heavy attendance.
